🎮 About the Game

Sonic.EXE Chaos Disasters is an intense asymmetric 1 vs All multiplayer experience heavily inspired by the Sonic the Hedgehog universe. One player takes the role of a deadly Killer, while the rest play as agile Survivors aiming to outsmart, distract, and survive the nightmare.


Beyond traditional Sonic characters, the game features special original and crossover characters, custom skin kits, and unique gameplay mechanics running on the Aeternus Engine.

🩸 KILLERS

Kolossos

Killer • Heavy Brute

A massive, unstoppable force that crushes Survivors through raw strength and relentless offensive pressure.

🩸 Abilities & Moveset

  • Charge: Dashes forward aggressively to slam into Survivors. Damage: 40 Cooldown: 20s
  • Slash: A swift melee swipe attack. Damage: 25 Cooldown: 3s
  • Grab: Grabs a Survivor, holds them, and throws them anywhere on the map. Damage: 10 Cooldown: 60s
  • Laugh: Taunts nearby Survivors with an intimidating laugh. Utility / Taunt
🌟 UPCOMING SURVIVORS

Starlaxy

Upcoming Survivor • Utility & Support

A futuristic survivor equipped with cosmic and spatio-temporal technology to manipulate position and assist teammates.

🌀 Passive Ability: Custom Skin Arsenal

Every single skin equipped by Starlaxy grants a completely unique moveset and visual theme. While each kit follows the core default playstyle structure (Teleport / Dash / Stealth / Slow), every skin features custom mechanics and specialized effects.
